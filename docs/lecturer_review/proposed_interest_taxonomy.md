# Proposed interest taxonomy (23 interests)

Source: `docs/interest_taxonomy_second_review.md` (second review, 2 November 2026), sections B2, D and "Interest descriptions". The interest names, explanations, examples and related skills are copied from that report without change. This is a proposal for lecturer review. It is not in use in the application and has not been approved.

## Column notes

- **Interest ID:** neither audit assigns IDs to interests. Every row therefore says "Not specified in the audit."
- **RIASEC group:** the second review places each interest under one primary Holland type, for display only. The six types are official O*NET constructs. The placement of this project's interests under them is the second review's interpretation, not O*NET data. Secondary links are listed where the second review gives them.
- **Source or rationale:** the reason given in the second review's decision table (section B2). Punctuation is adjusted (semicolons replaced by commas or full stops). The meaning is unchanged.
- **Status:**
  - Retained: kept from the first audit's 21 terms (wording unchanged apart from a capital first letter).
  - Renamed: same idea as a first-audit term, new wording.
  - Split or replaced: one of the 4 terms that come from the 3 first-audit terms that were split or replaced.
  - New: no first-audit equivalent.
- **Careers:** how many of the 64 careers carry the interest under the second review's recommended assignments (counted from section C).
