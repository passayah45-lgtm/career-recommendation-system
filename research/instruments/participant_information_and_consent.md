## What the study is about

We are testing a computer system that suggests careers based on a person's skills, interests and education, and shows which skills they could learn next. We want to know whether its suggestions agree with experienced reviewers and whether students find its explanations useful.

## What you will be asked to do

Depending on the part of the study you join:

1. **Profile part (about 10 minutes).** Describe your skills, interests, highest education and the career direction you are considering, in your own words. Your answers will later be read by independent reviewers who will judge which careers suit the profile.
2. **App part (about 20 minutes).** Enter your profile in the app, look at two versions of your career list, and answer short questions about each. Then rate the suggested skills to learn.

## Who can take part

Students of KIIT University aged 18 or older. You cannot take part if you are under 18.
