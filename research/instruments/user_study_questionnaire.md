# User study questionnaire (DRAFT)

Status: draft. Use only after ethics approval and signed consent. Items are adapted in spirit from the ResQue framework (Pu, Chen and Hu, 2011) and from the explanation aims described by Tintarev and Masthoff (2012); final wording should be checked by the lecturer.

Participant code: ________   Order: A then B / B then A (counterbalanced)

## Part 1. After each version of the career list

Version A shows ranked careers only. Version B shows ranked careers with the score breakdown and the matched and missing skills.

Rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

## Part 2. Skill gap for your top career

For each skill listed as missing for your top career:

| Skill | I already have it | Useful for me to learn | Not relevant to me |
|---|---|---|---|

For each linked learning resource:

| Resource | Opened and usable | Opened but not usable | Did not open |
|---|---|---|---|

Overall, the skill-gap advice was useful to me (1 to 5): ____

## Part 3. Open questions

1. What, if anything, was confusing about the explanations?
2. What was missing that would help you choose a career?

## Analysis plan (for the researcher)

- T1, T2, R1, A1, C1: Wilcoxon signed-rank test, version B vs version A, Holm correction across the five items.
- Gap precision: share of missing skills rated "useful to learn", with a bootstrap interval.
- Gap false alarms: share rated "I already have it".
- Overall usefulness: one-sample Wilcoxon test against 3.
- Open answers: thematic coding by two coders.
