# Student profile collection form (DRAFT)

Status: draft. Use only after ethics approval and signed consent. The form collects no names or identifiers.

Participant code (assigned by researcher): ________

1. **Your skills.** List the skills you have, in your own words. Include technical skills, tools, languages and other abilities. Example: "Python, Excel, teaching children, Photoshop".
2. **How you use them.** In one or two sentences, describe something you have done with these skills (a project, job, course or hobby).
3. **Your interests.** What kinds of work or topics interest you? Use your own words.
4. **Highest completed education.** Choose one: no formal qualification / secondary school / certificate or diploma / bachelor's degree / master's degree or higher.
5. **Current study programme.** Choose your school from a list (no course code or batch): [list to be added].
6. **Career direction.** Is there a career or field you are considering? (Optional)
7. **Anything else** that would help someone suggest careers for you? (Optional)

Notes for the researcher:

- Store answers exactly as written. Do not correct spelling or translate into catalogue skill names; the free text is part of what the study tests.
- Remove any name, email, phone number, roll number or employer name that a participant writes in a free-text field before the profile goes to reviewers.
- Profiles go to reviewers without any system output.
